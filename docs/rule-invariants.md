# Cancellation Hearts canonical gameplay invariants

These rules are hard engine constraints. UI, tutor, opponent policy, and simulations may not override them.

1. The first player clockwise from the dealer holding a 2♣ must lead the opening trick.
2. A player holding both 2♣ cards plays exactly one copy on the opening trick.
3. Every other player holding a 2♣ must play exactly one copy when their seat is reached.
4. If the final trick has no uncancelled card in the led suit, unresolved points are split between the two players in the highest-ranked canceling pair. Whole points are split as evenly as possible; any odd remainder goes to the earlier-played member of that pair.
5. A player may never contribute more than one card to a trick.
6. The opening trick may never contain a penalty-point card.

Opening legality consequence: after all mandatory 2♣ plays, players follow clubs when able. A player void in clubs may discard a diamond or a spade other than the queen of spades. Hearts and queens of spades are illegal on the opening trick. A post-pass hand is impossible only when a player holds nothing but hearts and queen(s) of spades; in that case, the hand is redealt before the opening trick begins.
